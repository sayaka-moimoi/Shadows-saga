13.Awakening Voice
---白中

"We fight with the light,
never to succumb to shadows.
Believing in the future, 
 …we sing."


Silent echoes in the night,
A voice calls out through endless light,
影の中で 彷徨う心
微かな希望が 胸を揺らす

Can I believe in a world so bright?
沈黙を破り 今叫ぶ
Your voice is calling me to fight,
導かれるままに

Awaken, the voice inside my soul,
Breaking silence, let it unfold,
光と影 交わる瞬間
この歌が運命を
この歌が運命を導く


Shadows whisper, pulling me in,
